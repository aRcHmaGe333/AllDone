# Promo text source

AllDone starts by removing grocery shopping. Once the same reusable container is also the thing food is stored in, served in and eaten from, other household work can disappear with it.

If food arrives in a container people actually like eating from, transferring it to a plate or bowl is often pointless. The container can be packaging, fridge storage, serving vessel and eating vessel at the same time. Fewer dishes are dirtied in the first place, while the returned container can be cleaned centrally in a system designed around minimum water, detergent and energy.

Ready meals push the idea further:

**receive → refrigerate → heat if needed → eat → return**

At that point shopping is gone, much of the stock management is gone, much of the dishwashing is gone, and routine cooking can become optional rather than compulsory.

The long-term point is not to give everybody the same industrial meal. It is the opposite:

**mass production without mass uniformity.**

Salt, spice, acidity, heat, texture, portion size, ingredients, religious restrictions, allergies and preferences can all become production parameters. One base dish can branch into household-specific versions without forcing every household to run its own kitchen.

That also changes what happens to food people actually love. A supermarket product disappears when the SKU no longer justifies its shelf space. A personalized production system can keep a meal alive because the people who want it still want it. It does not need national shelf economics to survive.

The same applies to home cooking. A good dish is often not one rigid recipe but a reliable way of improvising: a stable flavour structure, ingredients that can substitute for one another, and adjustments made by feel. A system that learns that structure can preserve the result without freezing it into one exact formulation.

The endpoint is not simply ready meals. It is personal food infrastructure: food produced efficiently at scale, but tuned to the household with more continuity than retail food can provide.
