## Project scope

AllDone proposes replacing recurring supermarket shopping with continuous, consumption-adjusted household food supply and a reusable-container delivery, collection, washing and refill loop. Existing supermarket sites could be progressively converted into storage and cooling, preparation, distribution and—where physically validated—Autonomous Food System production facilities.

Concept visualizations illustrate intended operation, not already-completed physical installations. The public repository documents existing work, prototypes, proposed engineering and validation needs.

This record distinguishes confirmed activity from intended contacts and proposed designs.
